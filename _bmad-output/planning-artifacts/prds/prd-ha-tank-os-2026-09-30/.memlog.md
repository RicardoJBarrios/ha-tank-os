- (decision) The product is initially a personal aquarium-management tool, with Veril as the first intended use case; open-source extensibility for future contributors is desired without assuming a first-release public support obligation.
- (decision) The product must bring manual and Home Assistant automated aquarium data together while retaining provenance; product and technical documentation remain English and the user-facing product must be multilingual.
- (assumption) The vision's proposed V1 core (Tank, Location, Method, Instrument, Sample, Observation; capture, correction, latest/history, export/import) is used as a draft boundary, not owner-approved scope.
- (event) Created substantial English PRD draft for owner iteration; unresolved scope and inferred requirements are tagged and indexed.
- (decision by user) The initial release must support managing multiple aquariums (Tanks); Veril is the first intended Tank, not the only one.
- (change) Updated FR-1 and proposed initial scope to require multiple Tanks in the first release; removed the corresponding open question.
- (decision by user) Aquarium measurement parameters must be configurable rather than a closed product-wide set; freshwater and marine aquarium types need editable starter templates. The product must also account for potential inferences from aquarium ICP results and replacement-water or salt-mix measurements, keeping derived information distinct from observations.
- (assumption) The draft proposes shipping editable freshwater and marine parameter templates in the initial release; exact starter contents and whether basic ICP/replacement-water comparisons are also initial-release scope remain open.
- (change) Added configurable per-Tank parameter requirement, editable freshwater/marine templates, variable ICP analyte support, and traceable derived-inference requirement; added initial-release scope question for basic comparisons.
- (decision by user) A use case is estimating the contribution of replacement saltwater from the salt-lot manufacturer's ICP analysis and source-water TDS plus preparation context. This is a reasonable but unreliable approximation, not a direct test; retain it with provenance and limitations for trend and issue review.
- (change) Refined FR-11 around Aquaforest salt-lot ICP plus TDS-based replacement-water contribution estimate, explicitly non-laboratory and approximate, with source and preparation lineage; formula and initial-release inclusion remain open.
- (decision by user) Each prepared replacement-saltwater batch must be individually traceable to its TDS source-water reading, salt product and specific lot, and that lot's ICP analysis when available.
- (assumption) The draft proposes including individually traceable prepared-water batch records in the initial release; the minimum required preparation fields and inclusion of the derived estimate calculation remain open.
- (change) Added Prepared Water Batch glossary entry, FR-12 traceability requirement, explicit link lineage to TDS and salt lot/ICP, and open question for minimum preparation fields.
- (decision by user) The initial release must calculate and retain the replacement-water salt contribution as an estimate, never as a real measurement. A comparable direct measurement always takes precedence over the estimate as the preferred/current value; the estimate remains separately available for trends.
- (change) Updated FR-11 and initial scope to include the replacement-water contribution estimate in V1; clarified direct-measurement precedence and retained method/input details as open.
- (decision by user) Each Prepared Water Batch must record at least source-water volume, salt type/product, salt quantity, and salt lot; retain traceability to source TDS and lot ICP when available.
- (change) Updated FR-12 with owner-confirmed minimum prepared-water batch data fields; further measurements/context are pending research and owner review.
- (event) Researched aquarium logging products, Aquaforest lot ICP preparation, conductivity/TDS interpretation, and community salt-mixing practices; recorded comparisons, candidates, source links, and estimation guardrails in addendum.md without elevating suggestions to approved requirements.
- (decision by user) Final salinity is recommended but must not block using a Prepared Water Batch; if absent, clearly warn that the batch has not been checked.
- (change) Updated FR-12 so batches can be used without final measured salinity only with a clear unchecked warning; measurements carry context, and estimates with missing required inputs are incomplete or unavailable.
- (decision by user) Each use of a prepared batch for a water change must record the amount transferred and destination aquarium, linked to that water-change record.
- (decision) Include a narrow Water Change Record in the initial release to preserve batch-to-Tank traceability; do not infer a general Aquarium Event or inventory-management system from this requirement.
- (change) Added Water Change Record glossary term and FR-13; batch transfer amount and destination Tank are linked, distinguished from prepared/source/removed volumes, and included in initial scope; removed the broad-scope open question.
- (decision by user) One prepared saltwater batch may be used in multiple transfers and water changes, potentially across multiple destination aquariums.
- (change) Updated FR-13 and addendum to allow multiple transfers from one batch across Tanks; any remaining-volume display must account only for recorded transfers/adjustments and disclose unrecorded-loss uncertainty.
- (decision by user) The same water-container tracking applies to evaporation top-offs, using freshwater or saltwater from an identified container, with manual or ATO use. Record container refill/fill level or observed consumption so the amount replenished can be calculated where supported; discards must also be recorded.
- (change) Expanded initial scope from saltwater batch/water-change tracking to freshwater and saltwater storage-container refill, level/withdrawal, discard, and manual/ATO top-off flows; derived amounts require adequate calibration/provenance.
- (decision by user) Marked water containers must support recording either a directly added amount (e.g. 1 L) or the current remaining volume (e.g. X L); these entry meanings must remain distinct.
- (change) Clarified FR-14 and addendum to support marked-container volume-added and current-volume entries, keeping direct inputs distinct from derived consumption.
- (decision by user) Top-off recording must support both optional ATO data when Home Assistant exposes it and complete manual recording when ATO data or volume is unavailable.
- (change) Updated FR-15 and addendum to make ATO ingestion optional, retain manual fallback, preserve provenance, and guard against duplicate records for one top-off.
- (decision by user) Manufacturer ICP reports must be manually associable with the salt lot in the core product; if Aquaforest retrieval can be automated, it must be an optional plugin/extension, never a core dependency. Manual use must remain available.
- (change) Added FR-16 and initial scope for manual manufacturer-report attachment/linking to exact salt lot; Aquaforest automatic retrieval is optional plugin-only, with supported manual report formats left open.
- (decision by user) Core manual ICP report association must support multiple forms: source link, report-file attachment, and transcribed results, combinable for the same report and salt lot.

- (decision by user) Trend timestamps: plot manufacturer ICP values at analysis date, prepared-water estimates at batch preparation date, and direct water measurements at measurement time; preserve issue/recording dates separately and never invent missing dates.
- (change) Updated FR-17 and addendum with owner-approved date semantics for ICP, batch estimates, and direct measurements, preserving distinct timestamps and missing-time uncertainty.
- (decision by user) The owner must be able to record direct measurements (e.g. salinity, KH, calcium, magnesium) of prepared water and link them to the exact batch; keep them separate from lot ICP and estimates, and prefer direct results for that batch.
